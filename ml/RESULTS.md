# Held-out results

Generated by `npm run ml:eval` on 2026-10-21. Model: 28-48-24-1, trained on 700 simulated episodes, alarm threshold 0.7.

**How to read this.** One *simulation step* is one second at demo speed. "Warned before breach" = the alarm was already on the step before a zone really went out of range. A *breach* is a zone truly out of its limits (noise-free). A *false alarm* is an alarm with no breach in the next 30 steps. Every detector only sees noisy readings (sensor noise up to 0.2 °C). Lead times are capped at 90 steps. The "zone-run" is one zone for one 360-step episode. Many incidents (a fire starting, a door left open) go from normal to out-of-range within a few steps, with no earlier sign in the readings, so no method can warn ahead of those. The AI's value is on incidents that develop over time.
